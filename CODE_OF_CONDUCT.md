# Code of Conduct

TraitTutor contributors are expected to keep collaboration respectful, source-grounded, and learner-safe.

Please:

- be direct and constructive in reviews;
- assume good intent while still asking for evidence;
- avoid harassment, insults, or discriminatory language;
- protect private user data, credentials, and unpublished materials;
- keep learner-facing claims modest and explainable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations or introduce unsafe product claims.
